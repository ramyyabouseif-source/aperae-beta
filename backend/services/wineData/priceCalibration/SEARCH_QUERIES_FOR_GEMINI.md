# Google Search Queries for Gemini AI - Wine Price Research

## Instructions

1. Copy each search query below (one at a time)
2. Paste into Gemini AI (or Google Search)
3. **IMPORTANT**: Look for the **CURRENT AVERAGE RETAIL PRICE (USD)** for the **SPECIFIC VINTAGE** mentioned in the query
4. Note the price from reliable sources:
   - **Wine-Searcher.com** (BEST - shows average prices from multiple retailers)
   - Wine.com (specific retailer prices)
   - Total Wine (specific retailer prices)
   - Producer websites (MSRP - Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price)
5. If the exact vintage is not available, note:
   - The closest available vintage
   - The price for that vintage
   - Any notes about vintage differences
6. Provide results back in the format specified at the bottom
